Welcome to AIZWAL

About AIZWAL: Aizawl is the beautiful capital city of Mizoram, located in the hills of Northeast India. It is known for its peaceful environment, clean streets, and friendly local people. The city sits on high ridges, offering stunning views of green mountains and valleys. Aizawl is also a cultural hub, filled with traditional Mizo heritage, local markets, and delicious cuisine. It’s a perfect destination for nature lovers and anyone who wants a calm and refreshing travel experience.
