ABOUT MARVEL:

Marvel started as Timely Comics in 1939 and later became Marvel Comics.
It created famous heroes like Spider-Man, Iron Man, Thor, Captain America, Hulk, and X-Men.
The Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) began in 2008 with Iron Man.

PEAK OF MARVEL(PHASE1-PHASE4)

Marvel’s Phase 1 introduced the main heroes and formed the Avengers.
Phase 2 expanded the world with new characters and bigger threats.
Phase 3 brought in heroes like Spider-Man and Black Panther and ended the Infinity Saga with Infinity War and Endgame.
Phase 4 explored life after Endgame, introduced fresh heroes, and opened the multiverse through new movies and series.

AFTER PHASE4:(MARVEL BECAME MID)

Phase 5 continues with Ant-Man: Quantumania, Loki S2, Deadpool & Wolverine, etc.
Focus is on Kang, multiverse threats, and connecting new characters.
Phase 6 will include major movies like Fantastic Four and Avengers: Secret Wars.
